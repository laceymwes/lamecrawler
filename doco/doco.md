# Lame Wizard DM Sim Summary Doc

### Summary
You, the Dungeon God (DG), influence/control dungeon masters (DMs) who manage a single-level dungeon, comprised of 4 chambers.
The each chamber has a specific function or purpose in the dungeon that generates a type of resource.
Only the DMs can traverse chambers.
Other NPCs stay in their respective chambers.
Chamber NPCs and equipment can be upgraded and added to effect resource generation.
The goal of a DM is to generate resources that can be given as tribute to the DG's avatar.


### Resources
All resources are sourced from the dungeon internals (non-visable).
There are three different classes of resources:
1. **Dungeon resources** that can only be consumed in the dungeon level
  - Research materials 
2. **Tribute resources** that can only be used as tribute to the DG avatar
  - Artifacts
3. **Transmutable Resources** are resources generated in the dungeon and can be converted to tribute
  - Gold
  - Manufacturing materials

### Levels
- Each level is a different "universe".
- Each level has a dungeon master you control or influence.
- Each level has 4 consecutive "cells" or "chambers":
  1. The dungeon master's chamber
  2. The barracks
  3. Engineering
  4. DG Avatar
